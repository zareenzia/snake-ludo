📖 How to Play

🎯 Getting Started

  • Quick Play vs AI — Jump straight into a game against a bot. Pick your color & avatar first!
  • Create Room — Make a room and share the code with friends to play online.
  • Join Room — Enter a friend's room code to join their game.

🎲 Game Rules

  • Players take turns rolling the dice. Click the Roll button when it's your turn.
  • Your token moves forward by the number you rolled.
  • 🪜 Ladders — Land on a ladder base and climb UP to the top!
  • 🐍 Snakes — Land on a snake head and slide DOWN to its tail!
  • Rolling a 6 gives you an extra turn (up to 3 in a row).
  • You must land exactly on square 100 to win. If your roll overshoots, you stay put.

⚙️ Settings

  • Click ⚙️ Settings (top-right) to change your name, color, and avatar anytime before the game starts.
  • Choose from 👦 Boy, 👧 Girl, or 🐱 Cat avatars — your face reacts during the game!
  • You can also rename the AI bot and pick its color.

😊 Emoji Reactions

  • When you land on a snake, your avatar cries 😭 and opponents laugh 🤣
  • When you land on a ladder, your avatar celebrates 😎 and opponents get jealous 😰
  • Expressions stay until the next dice roll!

🏆 Winning

  • First player to reach square 100 wins!
  • In multiplayer, the game continues to determine 2nd and 3rd place.
  • After the game, you can Play Again or Exit to Menu.

🛡️ Power-Ups (Coming Soon)

  • Shield — Blocks the next snake bite.
  • Double Jump — Doubles your next roll.
  • Reroll — Roll the dice again if you don't like the result.
